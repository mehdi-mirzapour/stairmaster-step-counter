🛡️ Privacy-by-Design & Edge Deployment Architecture

How our computer vision suite complies with GDPR and gym privacy standards through 100% on-device execution and dynamic face anonymization.

1. The Privacy-First Imperative in Gyms

Commercial gyms cannot legally record or store unblurred video of patrons without explicit GDPR Article 9 biometric compliance.

Our pipeline resolves this through four fundamental architectural safeguards:

  • Real-time Gaussian Face Anonymization: When facial keypoints (COCO #0–#4) are detected, an on-device 51x51 Gaussian blur and digital vignette mask identity.
  • Rear & Tread-Focused Camera Placement: The recommended camera perspective focuses on the stair exit and lower legs, not facial profiles.
  • Zero Video Persistence: Video frames reside solely in volatile RAM buffer and are immediately discarded. Only scalar metrics are exported.
  • 100% Offline Edge Inference: Solutions 2, 3, 4, and 5 require zero internet connection and make zero cloud API calls.
